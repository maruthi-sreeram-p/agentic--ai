STUDENT RULES AND GUIDELINES

1. Attendance
Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be allowed to write the semester end examinations. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% can apply for condonation by submitting a medical certificate or other valid proof to the Head of the Department, and a condonation fee of Rs. 500 per subject is charged. Students below 65% are detained and have to repeat the semester.

2. Library
The central library is open from 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM on working days and from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays. A student can borrow up to 3 books for 14 days using the ID card. A late return is fined Rs. 2 per day per book. A lost book must be replaced with the same edition or its current price must be paid. Silence must be maintained inside the reading hall and mobile phones must be kept on silent.

3. Hostel
Hostel in-time is 9:00 PM for girls and 9:30 PM for boys. Visitors are allowed only in the visitors' room between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM on Sundays. Students must take written permission from the warden before leaving the campus overnight. Cooking appliances such as induction stoves and heaters are not allowed in the rooms. Mess fees are collected at the start of every semester.

4. Fee payment
Tuition fee must be paid within the first two weeks of each semester through the online payment portal. A late fine of Rs. 100 per day is applicable after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs. 3000. Students who have not paid the fee will not get the hall ticket. Fee once paid is not refundable except in case of withdrawal of admission before the commencement of classes.

5. ID card and dress code
Students must wear their ID card inside the campus at all times. A lost ID card should be reported to the administrative office and a duplicate is issued for Rs. 200. On weekdays students are expected to follow a formal dress code. T-shirts with offensive slogans, torn jeans and slippers are not allowed in classrooms and labs.

6. Discipline and anti-ragging
Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offence under the UGC regulations. A student found guilty can be suspended or expelled and a police complaint can be filed. Students can report ragging confidentially to the anti-ragging committee or call the helpline number displayed on the notice boards. Use of mobile phones in the classroom during lectures is not permitted.

7. College transport
College buses run on fixed routes from the city to the campus and arrive before 8:45 AM. Bus passes are issued at the start of the academic year and must be shown to the conductor. The transport fee is paid once a year along with the tuition fee. Students who miss the evening bus at 4:30 PM can take the last bus at 6:00 PM only with a permission slip from the department.
