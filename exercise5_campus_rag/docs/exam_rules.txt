EXAMINATION RULES AND REGULATIONS

Eligibility and hall ticket
Only students with 75% attendance in all courses and no pending fee dues are eligible to write the semester end examinations. The hall ticket is released on the student portal one week before the first exam. A student must carry the printed hall ticket and the college ID card to every exam. A student without a hall ticket will not be allowed inside the examination hall; a duplicate can be collected from the Controller of Examinations office with a fee of Rs. 100.

Reporting time and conduct in the hall
Students must reach the examination hall 30 minutes before the start of the exam. Entry is not allowed after 30 minutes from the start of the exam, and a student cannot leave the hall during the first 45 minutes. Students must sit on the seat number given in the seating plan displayed outside the hall.

Prohibited items
Mobile phones, smart watches, earphones, calculators (unless the question paper says they are allowed) and any printed or written material are prohibited inside the hall. Bags must be kept outside. Only a transparent pouch with pens and pencils is allowed.

Malpractice
Copying, exchanging answer sheets, carrying chits or using a mobile phone is treated as malpractice. The answer sheet is seized, a report is made to the malpractice committee, and the student can be debarred from the examinations of that semester. Repeated malpractice can lead to dismissal from the college.

Results and revaluation
Results are announced within 30 days after the last exam. A student can apply for revaluation within 7 days of the result announcement by paying Rs. 800 per course. The revaluation result replaces the original marks only if the new marks are higher. A recounting of marks costs Rs. 300 per course.

Supplementary examinations
Supplementary examinations are held once a year for students who failed courses in the odd and even semesters. The fee is Rs. 600 per course, and the hall ticket rules are the same as for regular exams.
