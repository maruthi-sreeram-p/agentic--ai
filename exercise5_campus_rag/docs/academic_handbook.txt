ACADEMIC HANDBOOK

Credit system
The B.Tech programme follows a credit based system. A student needs to earn 160 credits over eight semesters to be awarded the degree. A normal semester carries 20 to 24 credits. Theory courses carry 3 or 4 credits, laboratory courses carry 1.5 or 2 credits and the final year project carries 10 credits.

Grading and CGPA
Marks are converted to letter grades: O (90 and above) carries 10 grade points, A+ (80 to 89) carries 9, A (70 to 79) carries 8, B+ (60 to 69) carries 7, B (50 to 59) carries 6 and C (40 to 49) carries 5. Below 40 marks the student gets an F grade and has to reappear for the course. SGPA is calculated every semester and CGPA is the weighted average over all completed semesters. A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is needed to graduate.

Internal and external marks
Each theory course is evaluated for 100 marks. 40 marks come from continuous internal assessment, which includes two mid-term tests, assignments and a quiz. 60 marks come from the semester end examination. A student must score at least 40% in the external examination and 40% overall to pass the course.

Course registration
Registration for the next semester opens two weeks before the semester ends and is done through the student portal. Add and drop of electives is allowed only during the first week of classes with the approval of the course advisor. Students with pending dues cannot register.

Attendance
A minimum of 75% attendance in every course is compulsory to be eligible for the semester end examination. Attendance is updated on the portal every week by the faculty.

Backlogs and improvement
Students who fail a course can write the supplementary examination. A student with more than 6 backlogs at the end of the second year is not promoted to the third year. Students can also reappear for improvement in up to 2 courses to increase their CGPA, within one year of completing the programme.

Internships and projects
Every student must complete one internship of at least 4 weeks after the sixth semester. The internship report is evaluated in the seventh semester. The final year project is done in the eighth semester under a faculty guide and is evaluated by an internal and an external examiner.
